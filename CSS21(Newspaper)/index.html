THE LONDON INQUIRER

June 8 1898

BAKER STREET ‘CONSULTING DETECTIVE’ EXPOSES SCOTLAND YARD BLINDSPOT IN DOCKLANDS MURDERS

Amateur sleuth locates missing heiress’s remains using "dust analysis" after police declare case cold.

By Jonathan Fletcher, Senior Crime Correspondent

LONDON — Scotland Yard faced severe embarrassment last night after a bizarre, freelance eccentric successfully resolved the high-profile disappearance of Lady Beatrice Cavendish—a case official investigators had abandoned three weeks ago.

The breakthrough came not from the ranks of Detective Inspectors at the Yard, but from the cluttered sitting room of 221B Baker Street. Mr. Sherlock Holmes, who styles himself as the world’s only "consulting detective," led authorities directly to a bricked-up alcove in an abandoned limehouse warehouse along the Thames, where the heiress's remains had been concealed.
